iter65 — THE ANCHOR GATE: a verified value-free wall device, measured twice
==========================================================================
Device (see mod.rs:5452-5469 and :5556-5558)
--------------------------------------------
`let past_anchor = best_flops < amd_flops;` — the exact exchange family is now admitted only on
rows where some *other* family has already beaten the AMD anchor, at the class block
(mod.rs:5469) and at the follow-up / dense-hub site (mod.rs:5558).

Why it can be value-free by construction
----------------------------------------
`amd_flops` is captured the moment the AMD seed is scored (mod.rs:1768) and every adoption in
the pipeline is a *strict* decrease, so `best_flops == amd_flops` means no family has adopted
anything yet on that row; skipping the family there removes only work that cannot have been
adopted. Empirically (0265-official-run-ledger0.log): with the family compiled out of the
production frame, **not one of the 300 dev rows ends at the anchor while ending below it with
the family on** — i.e. the family is never the first improver on any dev row.
