iter66 — THE CLASS-BLOCK WALK INSTRUMENT, AND THREE DEVICES PRICED IN THE GRADED FRAME
=====================================================================================
Frame of every number below: the *production* worker, `taskset -c 0-3`, one run per
row (ABBA pairing, 4 runs/row/arm), plus one official local sandboxed harness run
(300/300, a/b determinism gate). Probe-frame numbers are labelled as such.

Why this instrument exists
--------------------------
The lane's "waster" model was: some rows spend the ledger and accept only *internal*
window improvements that never reach the score (`0260-reserve-diff.txt`). Every stop
device built on that model (reserve, staged spend, plateau, idle sweeps) assumed the
signal "this spend did not pay" is observable inside the call — and none of them cut
the wall on the rows that carry the cap.

This instrument measures the thing directly, in the call, for free: `TripleWork::eliminate`
already calls `Game::eliminate` (which returns the exact `c` whose square is that pivot's
flop contribution) and a sweep *replays the whole ordering* it is scoring, so accumulating
`c*c` over the sweep's own eliminations yields the true Σc² of the permutation after that
sweep. The trailing window (never eliminated by the loop) is closed with uncharged
`game.eliminate` calls; `(charged_eliminations - mark) + tail == n` guarantees the measure
is complete before it is read. One `game.reset()` + a full seed replay gives the seed's own
Σc² — the quantity the caller's strict adoption test compares against.
Instrument (temporary, reverted before shipping): the tree at the moment of measurement is
`.scratch/iter66/instrument-tree.patch`; the trace is `XCHSEED` (per call) / `XCHTRUE`
(per sweep: n, seed, got, f, idle).

Consequence: no stop keyed on "the sweeps stopped paying" can ever fire on these rows —
the trajectory is monotone-improving right up to the charge abort. The cap-critical rows
are not rows where the exchange gains nothing; they are rows where the *first funded*
sweep already delivers everything the score keeps seeing.

FINDING 2 — the call is ended by the charge model, not by the sweep count.
`refine_window -> None` (probe refused) / `solve_component -> None` return
`changed.then_some(current)` immediately, so the schedule stops at the first window or
component the ledger cannot fund: 2 sweeps on methanol400/pooling_dt3, 3 on nd_netgen and
crudeoil_lee4_10, 4 on crudeoil_lee4_09, 6 on procurement1large/chimera/mpbp_48 — under the
shipped 3 GiB ledger. The ledger is therefore a *per-call wall budget*, spent on the
low-index windows first.

CAUTION recorded for whoever re-runs the instrument
---------------------------------------------------
The seed replay must be preceded by `game.reset()`: `pristine.game()` hands out a game whose
bucket links are only valid from a reset state, and a raw replay panics in `Game::blink`
(`bhead[-1]`, rgreedy.rs:497). With the reset the measure is exact and deterministic.

(killed)**; every second of class-exchange wall on a near-cap row costs ~0.5 % of hidden head-
room, and the 3 GiB rung's value (-1.83e-4 dev vs the 2 GiB rung, i.e. above the 1 bip
promotion threshold) is exactly what that headroom buys.
