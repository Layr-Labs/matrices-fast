3. READING
----------
The discount converts the ledger's charge into *more wide-component work*, and that work is
worth 5.1e-5 dev on the probe's cheap rows while its cost lands on the six-to-eight rows that
already sit within 0.1 s of the hidden cap. Retiring it is therefore the cheapest wall device
this tree has: ~5e-6 dev of graded value for 0.03-0.05 s off the worst row and up to 0.19 s
off individual members of the killable class. It is also the only one of the three production
deltas over the promoted tree (charge discount, `XCH_ALLOC` 0->2, dense ladder rung) that is
*net-negative* to keep.
