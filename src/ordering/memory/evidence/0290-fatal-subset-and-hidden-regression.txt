## C. The fatal-class subset instrument (new)

`.scratch/iter86/mksubset.py` + `runfatal.sh`: 55 rows = the 12 heaviest 4-core rows (W4.tsv), the
10 heaviest pinned-1-core rows (C1.tsv), every row with `nnz >= 10n, n <= 12 000`, plus `st_bsj2`,
`gasprod_sarawak16`, `chimera_mgw-c8-439-onc8-001`. Written to a non-canonical `SSI_CORPUS_FILE`,
so the official harness grades it on the *hidden* path (no census, redacted failures) and unlinks
it after loading; the runner regenerates it per invocation.

| arm (fork + registration + 6 sweeps base) | subset score | subset tiebreak | host wall |
|---|---|---|---|
| base (3 runs) | 0.7109, 0.7109, 0.7109 | 0.8743 | 49.7 / 51.5 / 53.3 s |
| dense-band rung ON | 0.7108 | 0.8742 | 51.5 s |
| AMF relabel `num_passes` 2 → 1 | **0.7115** (2 runs) | 0.8745 | 48.7 / 49.0 s |

* The score/tiebreak are exactly repeatable; host wall drifts ~7 % between runs minutes apart, so
  only ≥0.5 s wall deltas are readable.
* The AMF *pass* axis is a second lottery: its second pass is worth 6.0e-4 of subset score. The
  `RELABEL_BUDGET` doc table's "past 300 000 buys under 0.0002" is an AMD-budget-axis statement.
* Pinned variant (`PIN=1 taskset -c 0`) dies on the cap at **20.5 s**: time-to-kill is a usable
  margin proxy for the frame the cap is actually paid in.
